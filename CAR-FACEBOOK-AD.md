# FACEBOOK AD, DUSKWAY VISOR (car11.mp4, 14 seconds)

## Voice-over script (37 words, read at a normal brisk pace)

| Time | What is on screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 4s | Dark road, then oncoming full light | "One driver faces you with full light. And the road disappears." |
| 4 to 8s | The visor in hand, being opened | "Now see this shield. It clips on in seconds." |
| 8 to 9s | Daylight through the grey shield | "Grey for afternoon sun." |
| 9 to 12s | Full light, then the amber shield comes down | "Amber for full light at night." |
| 12 to 14s | Driver flips the shield | "Pay on delivery. Tap to see it." |

Say "And the road disappears" slowly, then pause for a moment before "Now see this shield". The pause makes people wait for the answer.

## Ad copy (primary text, 64 words)

One driver comes at you with full light, and for a few seconds you can't see the road.

In the afternoon, the sun does the same thing.

This 2-in-1 shield clips onto your car's visor in seconds. Amber for the night, grey for the day.

Delivery is free, and you pay only when it's in your hand.

Tap below to see how it works.

**Headline:** Full Light At Night? Flip This Down.
**Description:** Free delivery. Pay on delivery.
**Button:** Learn More, linking to `/car`

## Before you run it

- No deaths, accidents or FRSC numbers in the ad. Shock can get it rejected. They stay on the page.
- Don't say "no more glare", "night vision" or "100%". Say "softens".
- Seconds 2 to 3 of car11 look like the car is leaving the road. If Meta rejects the ad as sensational, cut that part first.
- The video has "The problem" and "The solution" typed on it with emojis, and bits of phone screen on the right edge. Ask your supplier for the clean file.
- Keep the supplier's written permission for the video. Meta can ask for it if somebody reports the ad.
