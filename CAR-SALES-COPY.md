### It's Not Your Eyes, And It's Not Your Driving

Plenty of good drivers start to think their eyes are going.

That's usually not it.

Your car's visor was made to block the sun from above you. But full light from an oncoming car doesn't come from above. **It comes straight at your eyes, through the gap under your visor.**

Pull the visor down further and it covers the road too. So the gap stays open, every single night.

And the light coming through it is getting worse. More cars now come with very bright white bulbs, plenty of drivers fit even brighter ones, and many don't dim their light when they see you coming.

You can't control the other driver.

But you can control what reaches your eyes.

### A See-Through Shield For The Gap

What you need is something that sits in that gap, that you can still see the road through.

That's exactly what the DUSKWAY 2-in-1 does.

It clips onto the visor you already have, and it holds two see-through lenses right at the level of your eyes.

**At night**, when full light is coming, you flip the amber lens down with one finger. It softens that harsh white flash, so it doesn't hit your eyes as hard and the road doesn't vanish on you.

**In the day**, when the sun is low and right in your face, you flip down the grey lens. It cuts the brightness, so you can see the car in front of you.

When you don't need them, both lenses fold up flat against your visor.

One thing we'll tell you straight, because most sellers won't: any tint lets in a little less light. So flip the amber lens down when full light is coming, and up again when the road is dark and empty.
