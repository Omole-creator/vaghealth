### It Isn't Your Eyes

A lot of drivers start to think their eyes are getting weak.

Most of the time, that isn't the problem.

The visor in your car was made to block light coming from straight above you. It's a solid board, so the further you pull it down, the more of the road it hides as well.

So you stop it halfway. And the glare comes in through **the gap between the bottom of your visor and your eyes.**

That gap is exactly where the low sun sits in the morning and evening.

It's also exactly where oncoming headlights shine at night.

And the nights are getting harder. More cars now come with very bright white LED lights, and plenty of drivers leave their full lights on, or fit brighter bulbs than the car came with.

You can't control what the other driver does.

And it's not your fault that your car came with a visor that only handles half the problem.

### What Actually Covers The Gap

What you need is something that sits in that gap, but that you can still see through.

That's the whole idea behind the DUSKWAY 2-in-1 visor.

It clips onto the visor you already have, and it holds two see-through lenses right at the level of your eyes.

You flip down the one you need. You still see the road through it. You just see it without the sting.

**The smoke-grey lens is for the day.** Think of it like sunglasses for your windscreen. It cuts the brightness of the low sun, so you can stop squinting and see the cars ahead.

**The amber lens is for the night.** When a car comes with full white lights, the amber lens softens that harsh white flash, so it doesn't hit you as hard.
